About Me

I want to master both front-end and back-end development so I can create complete and meaningful projects that people can use and appreciate. Every project I finish is not only a learning experience for me but also a step closer to my future goals. I aim to build a strong portfolio by completing my project checklist and eventually selling some of my peojects to profit and keep some to expand.
